RUMBLE

HOW TO PLAY

YOUR TEAM is three robots. The ★ one in the middle is your leader. If its head breaks, you lose.

EVERY ROBOT HAS FOUR PARTS: head, right arm, left arm and legs, each with its own armor. A broken part can't be used. Broken legs make a robot slow and easy to hit. Break the head and the robot is out.

HOW A RUMBLE FLOWS: robots wait on their command line (the blue dashes). Give one an order and it walks to the middle while it charges, does its move, then walks back to cool down. Faster legs walk faster. The fight pauses while you choose.

  • SHOOT: steady. BEAM: slow charge, goes through guards. STRIKE: fast charge, big hit, but you're easier to hit while charging.
  • GUARD (left arm): stand in front of an ally and take their hits until your next order.
  • LASER, MISSILES, SCAN, SHIELD, REPAIR: head moves with limited uses.
  • CORE: fills when you hit or get hit. Full core = a special move.

ARCADE: beat 20 rounds in a row. Every win unlocks a robot whose parts you can mix in the Workshop.

VS FRIEND ONLINE: one player creates a room and sends the 5-letter code, the other joins. Your games stay in sync move by move.
